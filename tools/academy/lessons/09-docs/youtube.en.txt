TITLE
Lira Cloud: your files on every device — and AI answers from them | Lira's AI Academy, lesson 9

DESCRIPTION
The clause you need is somewhere in forty files — and on your work computer. In Dewiar, files live in Lira Cloud: they're on all your devices, and Lira answers from them and shows which file the answer came from.

How it works:
• Drag files into “My files” → “Lira Cloud”: contracts, manuals, price lists — PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, text and images up to 25 MB.
• Ask by voice or text: “What does the Romashka contract say about deadlines?” — Lira answers and names the file and the clause.
• In the cloud, files are encrypted: only your own devices can read them; the server sees nothing but ciphertext.
• The model only gets the passages Lira found — not your whole archive.
• Files appear on their own at home, at work and on your phone (“Notes” → “My files”).
• Quick access: the little cloud tab in the notebook next to the widget — drop a file right onto it.
