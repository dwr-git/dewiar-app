TITLE
Lira for your company: staff, clients, website and leads in one place | Lira's AI Academy, lesson 30

DESCRIPTION
Can't see who's doing what, website leads get lost, clients wait for a call? In Dewiar it all comes together in one place: assignments to employees with photo reports, an assistant on your website and in Telegram, client cards, deals and a weekly summary. Half an hour of setup — and then your company runs itself.

How it works:
• Your company lives in the studio: “Brain” → “Create company”. One balance for everyone, and each employee has their own limit.
• An employee joins with a link: they open it on their phone — nothing to install. Assignments arrive in their “Work” section.
• Assign by voice: “Lira, tell Sergey to replace the wiring at the Ivanovs' by Friday.” The employee taps “I'll take it”, then “Done” with a photo; rescheduling needs a reason. An hour before the deadline, Lira reminds them herself.
• The board: who's doing what, what's waiting for your review, what's overdue. The same board is on the manager's phone.
• A website from a description, plus “Website chat”: the knowledge comes from your site in one click. The assistant answers clients day and night — on the site and in the company's Telegram bot, even when the computer is off.
• A client leaves a phone number — a card appears in “Clients”, and the lead instantly becomes a task for an employee: get in touch within two hours.
• Deals from lead to payment; who owes you is shown in red.
• Every Friday — the week in review: tasks given and done, who did how much, how many leads came in, and what's overdue.
• And every day Lira is an assistant for everyone: reminders, mail, documents, tasks — by voice, on the computer and on the phone.
• To start: Studio → “Brain” → “Create company”, then “Company launch” walks you through five steps.
