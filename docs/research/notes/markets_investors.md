# Markets and investor path for Dewiar ("Lira"), November 2026: Russia/CIS, English-speaking markets, UAE/Gulf, investors, regulation

Research date: 20 Nov 2026. Each number is dated. (est.) marks an estimate. Some search results came only as titles without page content; those are marked "title only".

## 1. Russia/CIS: usage, willingness to pay, app stores, payments, regulation

### Takeaway
By autumn 2026, consumer AI in Russia is mass-market. Mediascope puts monthly reach at 55% of people aged 12+ (Sep 2026). One player dominates: Yandex Alice AI reaches about 48%, roughly 9 in 10 AI users. GigaChat reaches 6% on Mediascope's definition, and ChatGPT is a niche, gray-zone service. About half of users say they would pay (around ₽2,000/month stated). The draft law that would have banned foreign AI was softened in June 2026, but the localization/sovereignty direction remains. RuStore and the Fast Payment System (SBP, Russia's instant-payment network) are now the main payment rails.

### Cited Findings
- **Audience (Mediascope, Sep 2026, population 12+; official sites and apps only, excluding messenger bots and AI answers in search):** 55% monthly reach for AI services, up from 26% in autumn 2025, so about 2x in one year. Alice AI: 59M people / 48%. DeepSeek: 13M / 13%. GigaChat: 6M / 6%. Daily use: 12% of the population. AI accounts for 1.3% of all internet time. — [Kod.ru citing RBC/Mediascope, 16 Oct 2026](https://kod.ru/auditoriia-neirosetei-vyrosla)
  - The absolute figures and percentages in that source do not reconcile (59M at 48% implies a base of about 123M; 13M at 13% implies about 100M). Use the percentages.
- **A year earlier (Mediascope, Oct 2025, reach):** Alice AI 14.3%, DeepSeek 9.4%, GigaChat 4%, ChatGPT 3.5%, Perplexity 1.4%, Character AI 0.8%. — [3DNews/Kommersant](https://3dnews.ru/1133039/trafik-iiservisov-v-rossii-vzletel-v-shest-raz-chatgpt-lidiruet-deepseek-stremitelno-dogonyaet)
- **Web traffic share (Digital Budget, Jan–Oct 2025, web only, apps excluded):** ChatGPT 39.9% (69.3% a year earlier), DeepSeek 27.8%, GigaChat 7.3%, Qwen 6.6%, Alice AI 5.7%. Traffic of the top 12 AI platforms grew about 6x year on year. Sber claimed 19M monthly GigaChat users (its own figure, late 2025). — [3DNews/Kommersant](https://3dnews.ru/1133039/trafik-iiservisov-v-rossii-vzletel-v-shest-raz-chatgpt-lidiruet-deepseek-stremitelno-dogonyaet)
  - Takeaway: ChatGPT dominates browser traffic among advanced and desktop users. Alice dominates reach because it is built into Yandex apps.
- **Microsoft AI Diffusion Q1 2026:** Russia is at 9.5% of the working-age population (8.0% in H2 2025), about 117th place. Belarus 9.6%, Kazakhstan 15.9%. — [Microsoft AI Diffusion Report 2026 Q1 (PDF)](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Microsoft-AI-Diffusion-Report-2026-Q1.pdf)
  - Inference: the methodology relies on Microsoft telemetry, so it systematically undercounts Russia, where Yandex and Sber dominate. This conflicts with Mediascope's 55%. For Russia, Mediascope is the better measure.
- **Willingness to pay (Avito Работы, 2,000 respondents, Jun 2026):** 55% of AI users say they are ready to pay for a subscription. Average stated willingness is ₽2,176/month; Gen Z (18–24) say about ₽3,493. By band: 28% up to ₽1,000, 14% ₽1,001–3,000, 6% above ₽5,000. — [ComNews, 25 Jun 2026](https://www.comnews.ru/content/246025/2026-06-25/2026-w26/1010/polzovateli-ii-gotovy-platit-za-podpiski-srednem-2-tys-rub-mesyac)
  - Stated willingness to pay usually overstates real payment.
- Counterweight: in a Sep 2026 survey, "70% of Russians use free AI" — [www1.ru, 21 Sep 2026 (title only)](https://www1.ru/news/2026/09/21/437403-70-rossiian-ispolzuiut-besplatnyi-ii-populiarny-u-grazdan-obycnyi-poisk-informacii-i-otvety-na-voprosy); "Most users said they are not ready to pay for AI content" — [RBC (title only)](https://www.rbc.ru/rbcfreenews/6ae9bd3e4be8a625cbdb68a0)
- **App stores and payments (TelecomDaily, 2,000 Android owners, 14–24 Oct 2026):**
  - About half paid for apps in the past year. Payment methods: SBP about 70%, bank cards 51%.
  - Main channel for game payments: RuStore 32% vs Google Play 23%. Google Play is still used more for installs (87% vs 82%).
  - 46% consider RuStore the more cost-effective store (18% say Google Play).
  - 40% would pay extra for AI photo/video features and 23% for text features; 44% would pay for no AI features.
  - Russian mobile app market (including App Store): about $3.41B in 2026 ($3.22B in 2025), forecast $4.67B by 2030.
  - Mintsifry (the Ministry of Digital Development) is worried about Google's new developer verification rules, which may restrict distribution of Russian apps.
  - — [Kod.ru, 2 Nov 2026](https://kod.ru/rustore-google-play-platezhi-igry)
- RuStore's monthly audience grew to 73M — [Bitrix24 journal (title only, undated)](https://www.bitrix24.ru/journal/news/rustore-auditoriya-73-mln/). Google's new rules "call into question" RuStore and sanctioned services — [RG.ru, 23 Mar 2026 (title only)](https://rg.ru/2026/03/23/novye-pravila-google-staviat-pod-vopros-rabotu-rustore-i-sankcionnyh-servisov.html)
- **AI regulation:**
  - The Mintsifry draft "On the basics of state regulation of AI applications" was published 18 Mar 2026. It introduced "cross-border AI technologies" (foreign models whose data leaves Russia), which could be banned or restricted. Services with more than 500k daily users would have had to keep Russian users' data in Russia for 3 or more years.
  - On 22 Jun 2026 a government commission approved a heavily revised version. The cross-border AI ban and the "trusted model" category were removed, along with the block of operator obligations. Labeling, liability and copyright were deferred to secondary legislation. Some provisions were expected from 1 Sep 2026; final adoption by the Duma was not confirmed in the source.
  - On 10 Jun 2026 the Duma rejected a separate bill on mandatory labeling of AI content.
  - Already in force: AI owned by foreign entities is banned in critical information infrastructure (KII), and AI for such facilities needs FSTEC/FSB certification.
  - Foreign AI services operate in a "gray zone" with limited payment options.
  - — [vc.ru (secondary source; verify against the bill text)](https://vc.ru/ai/3015854-zapret-chatgpt-v-rossii); also [Mentoday, 22 Jun 2026](https://www.mentoday.ru/life/news/22-06-2026/inostrannye-neiroseti-ostanutsya-zakon-ob-ii-smyagchili-poetomu-zapretov-ne-ojidaetsya/)

### Inferences
- In Russia, Dewiar competes for attention with Alice AI, which is built into Yandex Browser, Search and Yandex Station speakers and reaches nearly half the population. Head-on competition as a general-purpose assistant is not viable. The realistic positioning is "any model, including local, plus family plus desktop": Alice and GigaChat do not do task automation on a PC and do not let users pick a model.
- Dewiar's "any model" architecture (Yandex, GigaChat, local Qwen/DeepSeek) directly addresses the legal risk around foreign AI, a recommendation the vc.ru source itself makes to businesses.
- Payments should go through RuStore billing or SBP. Google Play billing and the App Store are not viable rails for Russian users (inference from the payment shares; Google Play's payment block was not directly confirmed in sources).
- A realistic price point is ₽299–990/month (est.). 28% are only willing to pay up to ₽1,000, and stated willingness to pay is inflated.

### Gaps
- No fresh 2026 figures for Marusya (VK) or the size of the voice assistant / smart speaker market in Russia.
- No official Alice AI / GigaChat subscription prices for 2026, and no confirmed share of actual payers.
- Final status of the AI law (whether the Duma adopted it) is not confirmed.
- No CIS-specific consumer AI data beyond Microsoft (Kazakhstan 15.9%, Belarus 9.6%).

## 2. English-speaking markets: adoption, payment, assistants, privacy/local AI, voice, family

### Takeaway
Adoption is high: UK 42%, Australia 40%, Canada 37%, US 31% of the working-age population (Microsoft, Q1 2026). India has huge absolute numbers but low per-capita adoption (17.6%). The market is concentrated in ChatGPT and Gemini, with Claude growing. Menlo reports that paying for AI became mainstream in the US in 2026, though the methodology needs checking. Voice, parents and older users are growing segments. Demand for local AI is real but developer-heavy (Ollama about 8.9M monthly developers).

### Cited Findings
- **Microsoft AI Diffusion Q1 2026 (share of working-age population using AI):**
  - Global average: 17.8% (Q1), rising to 18.8% in Q2 2026.
  - UK 42.2% (#8), Australia 39.5% (#11), Canada 37.3% (#15), US 31.3% (#21, up from 24th), India 17.6% (about #63, approximate).
  - Global North 27.5% vs Global South 15.4% (Q1); 28.8% vs 16.2% (Q2), so the gap is widening.
  - — [Microsoft Q1 2026 PDF](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Microsoft-AI-Diffusion-Report-2026-Q1.pdf); [Microsoft blog, 7 May 2026](https://blogs.microsoft.com/on-the-issues/2026/05/07/the-state-of-global-ai-diffusion-in-2026/); [Microsoft blog, 21 Sep 2026](https://blogs.microsoft.com/on-the-issues/2026/09/21/the-continued-state-of-global-ai-diffusion-in-2026/)
- **Menlo Ventures, "2026 State of Consumer AI"** (5,067 US adults, Morning Consult, Jul 2026; published 16 Sep 2026). Source: [Menlo Ventures](https://www.menlovc.com/perspective/2026-the-state-of-consumer-ai/)
  - Adoption and payment:
    - 64% of US adults use AI.
    - 55% of AI users pay for at least one AI product (about 90M Americans). The report compares this with "3% in 2025".
    - Typical spend is $20–49/month. 14% of payers spend $100+/month and account for 60% of spending.
    - Average spend: $55/month out of pocket, $95 when an employer or school pays.
    - Global consumer AI market: $40B in 2026 ($12B in 2025). 2.0B AI users worldwide.
  - Assistants used: ChatGPT 60% of AI users (45% a year earlier), Gemini 58% (36%), Claude 20% (7%).
  - Voice:
    - 19% use voice as their primary mode, 27% mix voice and text, and 46% speak to AI at least sometimes.
    - Alexa use fell to 22% (from 32%) and Siri to 20% (from 25%).
    - 25% of AI users aged 65+ count voice among their main ways of using AI.
  - Parents and family: 84% of parents use AI (vs 56% of non-parents), and 40% of parents use it daily. 34% of payers have a relative or friend who pays for AI.
  - Privacy: 76% of non-users cite privacy concerns, and 36% of users rank privacy/security among their top factors.
  - Growth areas:
    - AI for health questions: from 14% to 25%.
    - AI for mental health support: from 8% to 14%.
    - 41% have tried an AI agent and 24% use one regularly.
    - 16% have "virtual connections with AI".
  - **Conflict to check:** the jump from "3%" to "55%" probably reflects a definitional change. The 2025 figure measured the share of all users paying out of pocket, while 2026 counts "pays for at least one product", including payment by an employer or relative. Other sources: "about 3% of US households pay for AI" — [Bank of America Institute](https://institute.bankofamerica.com/economic-insights/consumer-ai-usage.html); [WJLA (title only)](https://wjla.com/news/nation-world/can-consumers-support-ai-just-3-of-households-are-paying-subscribers-bank-of-america-institute-analysis-artificial-intelligence); "about 2% of US households pay" (a16z) — [CryptoBriefing (title only)](https://cryptobriefing.com/us-households-pay-ai-services-a16z/). Treat household out-of-pocket payment as low single digits and the Menlo 55% as an upper bound.
- **Local AI:**
  - Ollama: about 8.9M developers use it monthly. Raised a $65M Series B (Theory Ventures; $88M total) in Jul 2026. 14 employees, about 176k GitHub stars. Paid cloud tiers run from free to $100/month.
  - Ollama says open models became capable of agentic tasks around Jan 2026.
  - — [TechCrunch, 9 Jul 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/09/popular-open-source-ai-developer-tool-ollama-raises-65m-grows-to-nearly-9m-users/)
  - Another source claims "52M downloads" — [Vucense (title only, secondary)](https://vucense.com/ai-intelligence/local-llms/ollama-hits-52-million-downloads-local-ai/)

### Inferences
- The English-speaking market has high adoption and is used to paying $20/month, but the Big 3 (ChatGPT/Gemini/Claude) hold the general-assistant slot. Alexa and Siri losing share in voice is an opening for new voice assistants.
- The best-supported niches: (a) privacy-first / local, given 76% of non-users cite privacy; (b) older adults and voice (25% of 65+ use voice) plus parents (84% use AI) for a "family network"; (c) agents that do tasks (24% use agents regularly).
- Local AI users are mostly developers (Ollama is a developer tool). Mainstream demand for "local" is expressed through privacy concerns, not through installing models. The product should hide the complexity of local models.
- India: high volume but 17.6% per-capita adoption and low ARPU (est.). It is a user-growth market, not a revenue market.

### Gaps
- No figures for LM Studio, Jan or GPT4All users.
- No separate share of paying users in the UK, Canada, Australia or India.
- No 2026 figure for the "AgeTech" / elderly care tech market.
- Microsoft Copilot consumer share was not reported in Menlo 2026.

## 3. UAE/Gulf: adoption, programs, free zones, Arabic AI, diaspora

### Takeaway
The UAE leads the world in AI adoption: 70.1% in Q1 2026 and 73.3% in Q2 2026. Government AI programs focus on government services. Hub71 gives a small check but is very selective (1.1%) and requires a presence in Abu Dhabi. Arabic AI (Jais 3, Oct 2026) is aimed at developers and government rather than consumers. There are no reliable 2026 numbers on the Russian-speaking diaspora.

### Cited Findings
- **Adoption:**
  - UAE 70.1% of the working-age population (#1, Q1 2026), then 73.3% in Q2 2026 (Singapore 64.3%).
  - Gulf peers (Q1 2026): Qatar 41.8% (#10), Saudi Arabia 29.4% (about #30), Kuwait 21.1%.
  - Saudi Arabia was the fastest riser in Q2 2026, from 30th to 25th place.
  - — [Microsoft Q1 PDF](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Microsoft-AI-Diffusion-Report-2026-Q1.pdf); [Microsoft, 21 Sep 2026](https://blogs.microsoft.com/on-the-issues/2026/09/21/the-continued-state-of-global-ai-diffusion-in-2026/); [Gulf News, 11 Oct 2026](https://gulfnews.com/uae/uae-ranks-first-globally-in-ai-adoption-among-working-age-population-at-701-per-cent-1.500706244)
- **Government programs:**
  - The AI Agent project aims to move 50% of government services onto AI agents within two years.
  - Abu Dhabi plans to train 20,000 government employees by the end of 2026 and to be an "AI-native government" by 2027.
  - A Federal Authority for AI and Data was announced.
  - Agent programs are also running in Dubai, Sharjah and Ajman.
  - — [Gulf News, 11 Oct 2026](https://gulfnews.com/uae/uae-ranks-first-globally-in-ai-adoption-among-working-age-population-at-701-per-cent-1.500706244)
- **Hub71 Cohort 18 (4 Jun 2026):**
  - 27 startups selected from 2,453 applications in 112 countries (1.1%). All were headquartered outside the UAE. Stages range from pre-seed to Series A.
  - Package: up to AED 250k in kind plus AED 250k in cash via a SAFE (up to AED 500k total, about $136k).
  - Requires licensing and operations in Abu Dhabi.
  - The cohort's startups had raised AED 31.2M on average before joining. The Hub71 ecosystem now has 525 companies.
  - — [Abu Dhabi Media Office](https://www.mediaoffice.abudhabi/en/technology/hub71-selects-27-startups-for-cohort-18-in-first-all-international-intake/)
  - A Nov 2025 report mentions an "enhanced" package of up to AED 750k — [TechAfricaNews (title only)](https://techafricanews.com/2025/11/07/hub71-launches-enhanced-incentive-program-offering-up-to-aed-750000-for-startups/). This conflicts with the AED 500k cited for Cohort 18.
- **Arabic AI:**
  - Jais 3 (MBZUAI IFM plus Inception42, with Cerebras; 15 Oct 2026) is six open models from 0.9B to 375B parameters with 512k-token context. It covers MSA plus Emirati, Gulf and North African dialects.
  - Jais Speech recognizes dialects from 19 countries and handles code-switching with English and French.
  - Distribution is via Hugging Face for developers, government and enterprise. No consumer app or user numbers are given.
  - — [Gulf News, 15 Oct 2026](https://gulfnews.com/uae/uae-launches-jais-3-arabic-native-ai-for-speech-writing-and-agents-1.500711935)
- **Russian-speaking diaspora:**
  - Russian is the "third language" in Dubai Marina after English and Arabic. Thousands left Russia over mobilisation. Russians may arrive again if a new mobilisation happens in autumn 2026. Regional conflict (Iran) is slowing Russian business activity in the Gulf.
  - — [AGBI, Jul 2026](https://www.agbi.com/people-lifestyle/2026/07/moscows-tense-mood-reaches-dubais-marinagrad/); [bne IntelliNews (title only)](https://www.intellinews.com/russian-businesses-in-middle-east-slow-activity-as-conflict-disrupts-deals-434258/); "Russians stranded in the Middle East scramble to leave Gulf" — [bne IntelliNews (title only)](https://www.intellinews.com/russians-stranded-in-the-middle-east-scramble-to-leave-gulf-429710/)

### Inferences
- The UAE has very high adoption but a small population (about 10M, est.) that is mostly expatriate. It suits a base for a company, investors and English-speaking pilots, not mass B2C.
- The Russian-speaking UAE audience is a natural bridge from the Russian product to the global one, but its size is not measured. Geopolitical risk (Iran conflict in 2026) adds volatility.
- An Arabic voice assistant on open Jais 3 / Jais Speech models is technically feasible for Dewiar ("any model"). Note, however, that the project's strict feature filter argues against adding a language until there is demand.

## 4. Investors: climate, checks, valuations, metrics, accelerators, solo founders

### Takeaway
At pre-seed in the US, AI captures about half of all dollars. The average SAFE grew to about $276k (Q2 2026). The market is barbell-shaped: a few large rounds and many small ones. Consumer AI is back in favor, but large seed bets fail more often. YC reports that about 1 in 5 companies is solo-founded, so a single founder is no longer a blocker. Investors look for retention that flattens by M3 and for real revenue.

### Cited Findings
- **Carta, Q2 2026 (US startups on Carta):** more than 11,500 pre-seed instruments; $3.19B invested; average instrument $276k (+27% year on year). AI took 49% of pre-seed dollars in H1 2026. At the 90th percentile, caps on SAFEs over $2.5M reach about $100M. — [Carta, State of Pre-Seed Q2 2026](https://carta.com/data/state-of-pre-seed-q2-2026/)
  - Pre-seed shows a "disappearing middle" (barbell market) — [Carta (title only)](https://carta.com/data/disappearing-middle-pre-seed-market/)
  - "AI seed startups are commanding higher valuations" — [TechCrunch, 31 Mar 2026 (title only)](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/31/its-not-your-imagination-ai-seed-startups-are-commanding-higher-valuations)
  - Median caps and valuations by round size were not obtained.
- **PitchBook Q2 2026 "State of Consumer AI":**
  - Consumer AI seed failure rate: 16.1% by company count, but 34.8% by dollars (the largest checks failed). Annualized seed returns: 93.4%.
  - Example: Yupp ($33M seed from a16z crypto, 2024) shut down in Mar 2026 due to lack of product-market fit.
  - Analyst view: seed is becoming more expensive and investor ownership is shrinking. Consumer startups compete with "the most entrenched platforms in history" and with frontier labs.
  - — [Yahoo Finance/PitchBook, 2 Jun 2026](https://finance.yahoo.com/sectors/technology/articles/consumer-ai-biggest-seed-bets-214200272.html)
- **Retention (a16z, 10 Sep 2025):** for AI companies, the retention curve "most often begins to flatten around M3". The M12/M3 ratio is used as the leading indicator, and top players head toward more than 100% net dollar retention. The sample is AI companies with more than $1M ARR, not consumer only; the numeric thresholds are in images and were not extracted. — [a16z, AI retention benchmarks](https://a16z.com/ai-retention-benchmarks/)
- **Solo founders:** YC reports that "nearly one in five" of the companies it works with is solo-founded. "AI lets one founder ship software with less labor." — [RuntimeWire, 17 Sep 2026](https://runtimewire.com/article/yc-solo-founders-startups-bits-atoms-2026)
  - Analyses of 2026 batches report that "consumer is fading" and half the batch builds AI agents — [DEV Community (title only, secondary)](https://dev.to/eliofbm/i-analyzed-all-478-yc-2026-startups-consumer-is-fading-half-the-batch-builds-ai-agents-hardware-51hi)
- **Notable consumer AI rounds (examples):**
  - First Voyage (AI habit-building companion): $2.5M seed — [TechCrunch, 15 Dec 2025 (title only)](https://techcrunch.com/2025/12/15/first-voyage-raises-2-5m-for-its-ai-companion-helps-you-build-habits/)
  - Eigen: seed led by Benchmark — [Fortune, 16 Apr 2026 (title only)](https://fortune.com/2026/04/16/exclusive-eigen-seed-round-benchmark-capital-ben-silbermann-mutual-friend/)
  - Wabi: $55M at a $1.25B valuation — [Magica (title only, secondary)](https://magica.com/news/wabi-raises-55m-at-1-25b-valuation)

### Inferences
- A realistic first round for Dewiar (est.): a $250–500k pre-seed via a SAFE. Options are an accelerator (YC standard deal, Hub71 AED 250k cash, Antler) or angels. Without traction, a valuation above about $5–10M cap is unlikely (est., based on Carta averages; no direct figure).
- Investors will need, at minimum: M1/M3 retention curves by cohort (a flattening curve by M3), DAU/MAU, share of paying users and ARPU, and organic growth. The weak points of a solo founder are the bus factor and how the founder is spending effort. A product built with AI is now neutral to positive (YC: 1 in 5 solo).
- Russian origin: Western funds in 2026 typically require the operating entity and IP to be outside Russia. This is not confirmed by sources in this pass; see Gaps. A UAE or other non-Russian entity (for example ADGM via Hub71) is a natural path for the global product.

### Gaps

## 5. Legal and regulatory risk for AI assistants/companions (2026)

### Takeaway
In the US, the key risk is companion laws for minors: California SB 243 has been in force since 1 Jan 2026, and New York signed a law in late Oct 2026. Productivity assistants and simple voice assistants are excluded from SB 243, but "characters" that sustain a relationship can fall under it. In the EU, Article 50 of the AI Act (disclose to users that they are talking to an AI, mark synthetic content) has applied since 2 Aug 2026; high-risk obligations were pushed to Dec 2027 and Aug 2028.

### Cited Findings
- **California SB 243 (in force 1 Jan 2026):**
  - Scope: "companion chatbots" with human-like adaptive answers that meet social or emotional needs and sustain a relationship across sessions.
  - Exclusions: customer service, business and productivity bots; game bots with restrictions; and standalone voice assistants that do not sustain a relationship and do not elicit emotional responses.
  - Requirements:
    - Disclose that the user is talking to an AI if a reasonable person might think it is human.
    - Have a suicide/self-harm protocol that refers users to crisis services, and publish the protocol on the website.
    - Warn that the bot may be unsuitable for some minors.
    - For known minors: a reminder every 3 hours that this is AI, and blocking of sexual content.
  - Annual reporting to the Office of Suicide Prevention from 1 Jul 2027. Users have a private right of action (injunctive relief, damages, attorneys' fees).
  - The federal executive order on preemption of state AI laws is mentioned.
  - — [Perkins Coie](https://perkinscoie.com/insights/update/california-companion-chatbot-law-now-effect); [TechCrunch, 13 Oct 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/13/california-becomes-first-state-to-regulate-ai-companion-chatbots)
- **New York:** Gov. Hochul signed a law (late Oct 2026) that defines about a dozen "unsafe AI companion features" and bans them in interactions with users under 18. The effective date and penalties are not in the available text. — [Broadband Breakfast/AP, 29 Oct 2026](https://broadbandbreakfast.com/new-york-enacts-ban-on-unsafe-chatbot-behavior-with-kids-as-some-states-push-ai-regulation/); bill [S9051-B](https://legislation.nysenate.gov/pdf/bills/2025/S9051B)
  - California and New York are taking "diverging paths" in the second wave of chatbot regulation — [FPF (title only)](https://fpf.org/?p=251239). The FTC is running an inquiry into AI chatbots — [Bloomberg Law (title only)](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/before-the-bar/the-ftcs-inquiry-into-chatbots-signals-broader-ai-crackdown)
- **EU AI Act:**
  - Since 2 Aug 2026, Article 50 applies and is enforceable: providers of chatbots and virtual assistants must inform users they are interacting with an AI unless this is obvious. Generated audio, images, video and text must be marked in a machine-readable format. Deepfakes must be disclosed.
  - Generative systems already on the market before 2 Aug 2026 have until 2 Dec 2026 to comply with marking.
  - Fines for Article 50: up to €15M or 3% of turnover.
  - AI Omnibus (Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, in force 27 Jul 2026): high-risk Annex III obligations move to 2 Dec 2027 and Annex I to 2 Aug 2028. The AI literacy duty was softened. New bans on nudification and CSAM-generating systems apply from 2 Dec 2026 (fines up to €35M or 7%).
  - — [Goodwin, Aug 2026](https://www.goodwinlaw.com/en/insights/publications/2026/08/alerts-technology-dpc-eu-ai-act-transparency-obligations-now-in-force)

### Inferences
- Dewiar's characters (Lira and others) remember the user and support emotional conversation, so they are likely to fall under SB 243 and the New York law. Exclusions apply only to productivity bots and simple voice assistants. Before launching in the US, Dewiar needs:
  - an AI disclosure,
  - a crisis protocol published on the website,
  - an age gate or a minor mode (3-hour reminders, content restrictions),
  - planning for 2027 reporting.
- For the EU, an "I am an AI" disclosure on first launch and machine-readable marking of synthesized voice and audio (Article 50) are needed now. On-device processing reduces GDPR risk but does not remove Article 50.
- Each requirement should be backed by a test or exam scenario, following the project rule that every user-reported bug becomes a test.
