# Consumer personal AI assistants & AI companions, 2025–2026: leaders, failures, lessons (as of 20 Nov 2026)

Notes for the Dewiar/"Лира" strategy. Every number has its date and source. "Third-party estimate" = not company-reported. Many secondary "statistics" sites (whizi, fatjoe, roborhythms, pocketanimus, presenc) are aggregators: I use them only where they cite a primary document, and say so.

## 1. Current scale and economics of the leaders and notable players

### Takeaway
Consumer AI is extremely concentrated: ChatGPT (1.2B WAU, Sep 2026; >50M consumer payers, Feb 2026) is several times larger than any rival on deliberate-use metrics, Gemini/Meta AI/Copilot inflate their numbers via embedded surfaces, and paid penetration is still low (4.5% of eligible US consumers pay ChatGPT/Gemini/Claude, Aug 2026). Companion apps are a tiny revenue pool (~$120M worldwide in 2025) with huge time-spent and terrible unit economics — the category leader Character.AI went into Chapter 11 in Oct 2026.

### Inferences
- "Deliberate-use" leaders (open-the-app) are ChatGPT ≫ Gemini > Claude/Grok/Perplexity; Meta AI/Copilot/Alexa numbers are distribution artifacts. A small product cannot win on general Q&A; it wins only where the incumbents' product shape is wrong (voice-first home presence, family, character, privacy).
- Companion revenue per user is tiny: Character.AI earned ~$0.44/MAU/month (Aug 2026: $5.3M/12M) while users spent ~60 h/month — inference cost scales with *time*, revenue does not. Talkie's ~$1.4/payer/month confirms low ARPPU in the anime-character segment.
- Price anchors in 2026: $8 (ChatGPT Go) / $7.99 (Google AI Plus) entry, $20 standard, $100–$300 power tiers; Alexa+ "free with an existing subscription" ($19.99 otherwise). A new assistant priced above ~$8–10 for mass users fights the anchors.

## 2. Product mechanics that drove retention / word of mouth — and in-chat features that failed

### Takeaway
What moved the needle in 2025–2026 were *capability shocks shareable as artifacts* (image models: 4o images, Nano Banana), *trust/values moments* (Claude's no-ads Super Bowl, DoD stance), and *habit surfaces where the user already is* (Alexa in the kitchen, OpenClaw inside WhatsApp/Telegram). Bolted-on "features inside the chat" (Pulse, Group Chats, Tasks, Shopping, Atlas, Agent) largely did not break through; memory/voice/search became table stakes rather than acquisition drivers.

### Cited Findings
- Hits 2025: ChatGPT 4o image gen drove up to 1M new users/hour at peak; Nano Banana 200M images in week one, +10M Gemini users; Sora reached 12M+ downloads but D30 retention <8% vs >30% for top consumer apps — [a16z State of Consumer AI 2025](https://a16z.com/state-of-consumer-ai-2025-product-hits-misses-and-whats-next/)
- Misses: ChatGPT Pulse, Group Chats, Record, Shopping Research, Tasks, Study Mode — "none have truly broken through" in usage or retention; Atlas browser: <5% of ChatGPT users visited its download page; ChatGPT Agent slow and buggy; Google AI Mode ~2% of weekly users; Google Portraits/Doppl/Whisk/Gems muted; voice, memory, web search, research are "table stakes" — [a16z State of Consumer AI 2025](https://a16z.com/state-of-consumer-ai-2025-product-hits-misses-and-whats-next/)
- Startups that broke out have "opinionated, focused interfaces" (Replit, Gamma, Character AI, Suno, ElevenLabs, Manus, Krea, Lovable) because labs push features through their existing general product — [a16z State of Consumer AI 2025](https://a16z.com/state-of-consumer-ai-2025-product-hits-misses-and-whats-next/)
- Alexa+: engagement up 2–3× in conversations, recipe use 5×, music +25% — gains came from everyday household routines, not new categories — [TechCrunch, 4 Feb 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/04/alexa-amazons-ai-assistant-is-now-available-to-everyone-in-the-u-s/); Panay lists lists/calendars, reading documents aloud, quizzing, ordering food/rides, smart home, sharing photos with family — [TechRadar, 10 Jul 2025](https://www.techradar.com/computing/artificial-intelligence/its-astonishing-to-watch-the-usage-patterns-on-alexa-amazons-panos-panay)
- Voice adds retention in companions: ElevenLabs reports +20% 7-day retention and +53% long (>5 min) voice calls among Replika paid users (vendor claim) — [AICompanionPick](https://aicompanionpick.com/replika-user-base-2026)
- Characters/avatars bring downloads, not money: Grok companions +40% downloads but only +9% revenue at launch (Appfigures) and were shut down within ~14 months for compute cost — [RoboRhythms](https://www.roborhythms.com/grok-companions-discontinued/)
- Proactive/people-memory is the bet of the relationship-focused players: Pi Journeys builds memory around the people in the user's life and nudges proactively (Jul 2026); Inflection cites users juggling ~2 AI tools daily and choosing on tone, emotional understanding, personalization — [VentureBeat, 22 Jul 2026](https://venturebeat.com/ai/inflection-ai-returns-to-consumer-market-with-pi-journeys-after-microsoft-upheaval/)
- Trust as growth lever: Claude's paid subs more than doubled in Q1 2026 after ads promising "no ads" and the DoD dispute; returning lapsed users hit a record in Feb 2026 — [TechCrunch, 28 Mar 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/28/anthropics-claude-popularity-with-paying-consumers-is-skyrocketing/?locale=en)
- Agents that *do things* show fast pull when they live in an existing channel: OpenClaw (messaging-app interface) reached ~38M monthly site visits; Meta Muse (personal agent) #1 US App Store 12+ days; Instinct 100K users in 3 weeks invite-only — [FatJoe](https://fatjoe.com/blog/openclaw-ai-stats/); [a16z 7th ed.](https://a16z.com/100-gen-ai-apps-7/)
- Abrupt persona change destroys trust: Replika removed erotic roleplay in Feb 2023 after Italy's order; a Harvard Business School working paper (25-018) on 3,072 vs 9,721 Reddit posts documented "identity discontinuity" grief; partial reversal for pre-Feb-2023 accounts on 24 Mar 2023 — [AICompanionPick, citing HBS/Vice](https://aicompanionpick.com/replika-user-base-2026). Same pattern at Character.AI: retirement of 9 legacy models (8 May 2026), metered swipes and full-screen ads (Feb–Apr 2026), petitions for old models; Grok companions removal triggered petitions/grief — [RoboRhythms](https://www.roborhythms.com/what-happened-to-character-ai/); [RoboRhythms](https://www.roborhythms.com/grok-companions-discontinued/)

### Inferences
- For Dewiar: characters with faces/voices are an acquisition hook, not a business model; they must be attached to a recurring job (day, reminders, family) to retain and to justify cost. Voice measurably lifts retention but also cost (Grok cut voice to 30–60 min/day).
- "Persona continuity" is a product promise: never silently change a character's voice/personality/model; versioning and opt-in upgrades are a differentiator against cloud companions that change under users.
- New features inside an existing chat rarely get discovered — ship fewer things, make each one visible in daily flow (consistent with the owner's "subtract, don't add" rule).

### Gaps
- No public cohort data on the retention effect of memory or proactive messages in general assistants (only vendor claims).
- No public data on streaks/gamification in AI assistants found.

## 3. What killed or crippled the failures

### Takeaway
Three killers recur: (1) inference cost that scales with engagement while revenue does not (Character.AI, Grok companions, Pi usage caps); (2) safety/minors liability and regulators making the company unsellable (Character.AI: 28 buyers declined; Grok bans; Replika €5M fine); (3) hardware with no job users repeat daily, sold at a premium plus a subscription (Humane, Rabbit, Friend). Incumbent "failures" (Siri, Copilot consumer) came from shipping late or forcing distribution without daily use.

### Cited Findings

- Child and elder modes are a liability magnet: minors + emotional characters = the precise fact pattern of the Character.AI suits and SB 243. ## 4. Trends 2026 and where incumbents are weak

### Takeaway
2026 is the year of "assistants that do things": desktop/computer-use agents (Claude Cowork/Dispatch, Perplexity Computer, OpenClaw, Microsoft Autopilot on OpenClaw), personal agents from platforms (Meta Muse, Siri AI with personal context, Alexa+), and voice in the home. Incumbents are weak on privacy/locality (ads arriving in ChatGPT, Meta), multi-model choice, character continuity, family/household multi-user design, and on non-English/regional markets where they distribute through bundles.

### Cited Findings
- Siri AI (iOS 27, Sep 2026) runs Apple models refined with Gemini on device + Private Cloud Compute, with personal context across photos, mail, messages, calendar — Apple sells privacy-preserving personal context as the feature — [TechCrunch, 3 Aug 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/03/apple-finally-fixed-siri-so-why-does-it-feel-anticlimactic/)

### Inferences
- Incumbent weak spots that match Dewiar: (a) privacy without ads — the only big-brand counter-position is Apple (Apple-only) and Claude (cloud); a cross-platform Windows/Linux/Android local-first assistant has no direct big competitor; (b) any-model including local — incumbents are locked to own models; OpenClaw proves demand but is developer-grade and insecure, leaving a "safe OpenClaw for normal people/families" gap; (c) household/family multi-user — only Alexa addresses it, and only in the US with Echo hardware; (d) persona continuity — cloud companions keep breaking it.

- The fastest-growing "assistant" surfaces live where people already are (WhatsApp/Telegram for OpenClaw, kitchen speaker for Alexa, the phone OS for Siri). A Telegram/WhatsApp channel for Лира is likely higher-leverage than new in-app sections.

## 5. Non-obvious structural insights (winner-take-most, multi-homing, DAU/MAU, retention curves)

### Takeaway
The market is "winner-take-most" with little multi-homing among payers (only 9–13% pay for more than one AI product), ChatGPT has a DAU/MAU (~36%) closer to a utility than its rivals (Gemini ~21%), AI subscription apps monetize better upfront but churn ~30% faster than non-AI apps, and spending is extremely concentrated in power users (top 1% of spenders = 19.5% of spend).

### Cited Findings
- Multi-homing: fewer than 10% of ChatGPT weekly users visited another major model provider for most of 2025; only 9% of consumers pay for more than one of ChatGPT/Gemini/Claude/Cursor (Yipit) — [a16z State of Consumer AI 2025](https://a16z.com/state-of-consumer-ai-2025-product-hits-misses-and-whats-next/); only 13% of people who pay for one AI product pay for another; only 8% of US ChatGPT subscribers also pay for Claude (Aug 2026) — [a16z 7th ed.](https://a16z.com/100-gen-ai-apps-7/)
- Counterpoint: users "juggle about two AI tools daily" (Inflection claim, Jul 2026) — [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/ai/inflection-ai-returns-to-consumer-market-with-pi-journeys-after-microsoft-upheaval/). Free multi-use exists; paid multi-homing is rare.
- DAU/MAU: ChatGPT 36%, Gemini 21% (Yipit, Dec 2025) — [a16z State of Consumer AI 2025](https://a16z.com/state-of-consumer-ai-2025-product-hits-misses-and-whats-next/); nearly half of US consumers use AI but only 25% daily — [a16z 7th ed.](https://a16z.com/100-gen-ai-apps-7/)
- Retention: ChatGPT M12 desktop user retention 50% vs Gemini 25%; M12 paid retention 68% vs 57%; Sora D30 <8% vs >30% top consumer apps — [a16z State of Consumer AI 2025](https://a16z.com/state-of-consumer-ai-2025-product-hits-misses-and-whats-next/)
- RevenueCat State of Subscription Apps 2026 (AI vs non-AI): annual retention 21.1% vs 30.7%; monthly-plan retention 6.1% vs 9.5% (12-month); trial-to-paid 8.5% vs 5.6%; download-to-paid 2.4% vs 2.0%; median refund 4.2% vs 3.5%; RLTV monthly $18.92 vs $13.59; AI apps = 27.1% of apps on the platform — [TechCrunch, 10 Mar 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/10/ai-powered-apps-struggle-with-long-term-retention-new-report-shows)
- Paid penetration: 4.5% of eligible US consumers pay ChatGPT/Gemini/Claude (Aug 2026, up from 2.1%); a16z State of Markets: 2.2% of US households pay for AI; ChatGPT ~3× the US paid subs of Claude or Gemini — [a16z 7th ed.](https://a16z.com/100-gen-ai-apps-7/); [TechCrunch, 9 Oct 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/09/a16zs-olivia-moore-on-the-state-of-consumer-ai/); Grok payers ≈1.6% of MAU (Mar 2026) — [Whizi](https://whizi.io/resources/grok-statistics/)
- Spend concentration (US card panel): top 1% of spenders = 19.5% of spend (> bottom 50% at 16.6%); top 10% ≈ half; top 1% average ~$903/month (+80% in 18 months); median payer ~$25/month. 44 AI-native web products: 84% subscriptions, 64% usage/credits, 14% ads — [a16z 7th ed.](https://a16z.com/100-gen-ai-apps-7/)
- Only 7 companies rank in the top group on web, mobile and revenue simultaneously (ChatGPT, Claude, Suno, Perplexity, Photoroom, Canva, Notion) — [a16z 7th ed.](https://a16z.com/100-gen-ai-apps-7/)
- Companion concentration: top 10% of companion apps take 89% of category revenue (2025) — [TechCrunch/Appfigures](https://techcrunch.com/2025/08/12/ai-companion-apps-on-track-to-pull-in-120m-in-2025)
- Consumer→enterprise drift: Gamma, ElevenLabs, Cursor became majority-enterprise within ~18 months — [TechCrunch, 9 Oct 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/09/a16zs-olivia-moore-on-the-state-of-consumer-ai/)

### Inferences
- Because payers rarely hold two AI subscriptions, Dewiar should not position as "another AI subscription" next to ChatGPT; it should position as the layer that *uses* the model the user already has (bring-your-own key/subscription, local models) — complementary, not substitutive. This also explains the "any model" value prop.
- High trial conversion but fast annual churn in AI apps argues for annual/family plans and for value that accumulates locally (memory, notes, family network) — switching cost from accumulated personal data, not from model quality.
- Power-user concentration suggests a cheap/free mass tier plus a small "business" tier (websites for small business, agent tasks) where spend concentrates, rather than a single mid-price plan.
- Companion-category math (top 10% take 89%; leader bankrupt at 12M MAU) means "character companion" alone is not a fundable/sustainable category for a tiny team; characters should be the face of a utility assistant.
